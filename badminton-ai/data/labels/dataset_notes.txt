LABELING METHODOLOGY
--------------------
- One frame labeled per shot — specifically the frame immediately
  BEFORE shuttle contact (pre-contact body position)
- Near-side player only (far player keypoints too noisy)
- Timestamps identified using custom Colab frame scrubber tool
- Labels assigned by manual visual inspection

KNOWN ISSUES & LIMITATIONS
---------------------------
* Backhands lumped into one category regardless of shot type
  (backhand drop, backhand clear, backhand drive all = 'backhand')
  Reason: insufficient examples to split further at v0.1

* 'net_shot' is a broad category — includes net kills, net lifts,
  and gentle net drops. First refinement target for v0.2.

* Smash and drop look very similar at the contact frame.
  The main visual difference is shuttle behavior post-contact,
  not player body position. Low accuracy expected on drop.
  Fix: sequence-based features (5-frame windows) in v0.2.

* Model is angle-specific — raw x/y keypoints are not
  viewpoint-invariant. Retraining required for different camera angles.
  Fix: convert to joint angles (rotation-invariant) in v0.3.

* Dataset biased toward two players (Axelsen + Momota).
  Generalization to other players' mechanics is uncertain.
  Fix: add 3+ more players in v0.2 dataset expansion.

* Amateur footage not yet tested. Pipeline likely to break on:
  - Different camera angles
  - Worse lighting
  - Lower resolution
  - Closer camera distance (player fills more of frame)
